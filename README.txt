BUBBY'S BIRTHDAY WEBSITE — QUICK PUBLISH GUIDE

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- index.html, style.css, script.js: the complete responsive birthday site
- assets/photos/: all 10 uploaded photos (lavender outfit photo is the final image)
- assets/bubby-song.mp4: the complete 2:19 recording with its original audio preserved
- assets/gallery-instrumental.mp3: an original gentle instrumental made for the photo gallery

PUBLISH FREE WITH GITHUB PAGES
1. Sign in at https://github.com/ (create a free account if needed).
2. Click the + menu → New repository.
3. Name it bubby-birthday (or another name), choose Public, then Create repository.
4. On the repository page choose Add file → Upload files.
5. Open this folder on your computer and upload its CONTENTS (index.html, style.css, script.js, README.txt and the assets folder). Do not upload the ZIP file into the repository; extract it first.
6. Commit the files to the main branch.
7. Open Settings → Pages.
8. Under Build and deployment, choose Deploy from a branch. Select Branch: main and Folder: /(root), then Save.
9. Wait a minute or two. GitHub will show your website address, usually https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/bubby-birthday/ .
10. Open that link on your phone and test the video and gallery music.

IMPORTANT
- The video is compressed to a web-friendly 640px-wide MP4 to keep the site light. Its full duration and original audio are preserved; it is not trimmed.
- The gallery music starts only after Bubby taps the play button (phones block automatic audio).
- GitHub Pages sites in public repositories are publicly accessible. Anyone with the link may be able to view the photos and video. Only publish content you are comfortable sharing publicly. You can instead use a private video host and replace the video source if you need more privacy.
- Keep the folder structure exactly as supplied. In particular, do not move the assets folder.
